## 2. Core Image Curation Directives
- **Zero Auto-Scraped Low-Quality Images**: Strictly prohibit blind, noisy web scraping scripts that pull irrelevant, low-res, or misattributed images.
- **Strict Visual Resemblance**: Every featured destination photo must authentically represent the exact monument, temple, fort, waterfall, or landscape of that specific district and state.
- **Pristine Image Registry**: Rely on verified high-resolution photography curated by state tourism authorities, ASI records, and human-verified archives.
