Globetrotter Gateway Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 | In compliance with India DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR

1. Data Minimization & Collection Purpose

Globetrotter Gateway operates on strict data minimization principles. We collect only data strictly necessary to issue circuit passes and coordinate travel itineraries across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana:

  • Account Data: Full name, phone number, and verified email address for pass transmission.
  • Pass Information: Selected circuit destinations, travel dates, and cryptographic QR ticket hashes.
  • Telemetry: Anonymized server logs without tracking across external websites.

2. Zero Third-Party Tracker Guarantee

We do not sell personal data, utilize cross-site tracking beacons, or bundle third-party ad retargeting pixels. All travel recommendations and routing calculations are executed locally or via sovereign server-side proxies.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

Our platform uses only essential functional cookies and local memory storage necessary to maintain active pass verification sessions and offline caching for connectivity blackouts.

4. Right to Erasure & Data Deletion

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and GDPR Article 17, visitors have the absolute right to purge their account and historical pass records at any time via their profile settings or by contacting privacy@globetrottergateway.in.
